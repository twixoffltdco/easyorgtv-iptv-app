Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Easyorgtv is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

Easyorgtv is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • Server Logs: Standard web server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and access times. These are retained for a maximum of 30 days for security and debugging purposes.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous, aggregated statistics about channel popularity and system usage to improve our service.

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Email addresses or account data (we have no accounts)
  • Viewing history or preferences
  • Payment information (the service is free)

2. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used for:

  • Operating and maintaining the service
  • Protecting against abuse and security threats
  • Improving service performance and reliability
  • Generating anonymous usage statistics

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Easyorgtv may use minimal browser storage for:

  • Remembering your display preferences (e.g., dark mode)
  • Improving navigation and user experience

We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting core functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

When you watch a stream through Easyorgtv, your browser connects directly to the stream source. We do not proxy video content. This means:

  • The stream provider may collect their own analytics
  • Your IP address is visible to the stream provider
  • Third-party privacy policies may apply to stream content

We recommend using a VPN if you have privacy concerns about streaming content.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights or the safety of users
  • In anonymized, aggregated form that cannot identify individuals

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Minimal data retention policies

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Use the service at your own risk.

7. Children's Privacy

Easyorgtv is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of any data collection
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Since we collect minimal data and have no user accounts, most requests are automatically satisfied.

9. International Users

Easyorgtv may be accessed from anywhere in the world. By using our service, you consent to the transfer of any data to servers located in various jurisdictions.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact us through the project page.
