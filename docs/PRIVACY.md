# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 2024

## Overview

Easyorgtv is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our service.

## Information We Collect

Easyorgtv is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

### What We Collect

- **Server Logs:** Standard web server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and access times. Retained for a maximum of 30 days for security and debugging.
- **Usage Data:** Anonymous, aggregated statistics about channel popularity and system usage.

### What We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information
- Email addresses or account data (we have no accounts)
- Viewing history or preferences
- Payment information (the service is free)

## How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used for:

- Operating and maintaining the service
- Protecting against abuse and security threats
- Improving service performance and reliability
- Generating anonymous usage statistics

## Cookies and Local Storage

Easyorgtv may use minimal browser storage for:

- Remembering display preferences (e.g., dark mode)
- Improving navigation and user experience

We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. You can disable cookies without affecting core functionality.

## Third-Party Services

When you watch a stream through Easyorgtv, your browser connects directly to the stream source. We do not proxy video content. This means:

- The stream provider may collect their own analytics
- Your IP address is visible to the stream provider
- Third-party privacy policies may apply

We recommend using a VPN if you have privacy concerns.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your information except:

- When required by law or legal process
- To protect our rights or the safety of users
- In anonymized, aggregated form

## Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures:

- HTTPS encryption for all connections
- Regular security updates and monitoring
- Minimal data retention policies

## Children's Privacy

Easyorgtv is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access any personal data we hold about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of any data collection
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Since we collect minimal data and have no user accounts, most requests are automatically satisfied.

## International Users

Easyorgtv may be accessed worldwide. By using our service, you consent to data transfer to various jurisdictions.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with an updated revision date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
